# Synopsys Benefits and Dental Questions

## Purpose

Track benefit and dental insurance questions before and after joining Synopsys.

This note is especially important because dental deep cleaning / scaling and root planing may be needed, and insurance timing could affect when to schedule treatment.

## Current Known Context

- Employer: Synopsys Canada
- Start date: August 17, 2026
- Role: Analog Design, Senior Staff Engineer
- Location: Mississauga, Ontario
- Dental treatment may include deep cleaning / scaling and root planing
- Need to confirm whether dental coverage starts immediately or after a waiting period

## Key Questions for HR / Benefits Provider

### Coverage Start Date

- When exactly does dental coverage start?
- Is coverage active from the first day of employment?
- Is there any waiting period?

### Dental Coverage

- Is routine cleaning covered?
- Is deep cleaning covered?
- Is scaling and root planing covered?
- Is periodontal treatment covered?
- Is a dentist's pre-authorization required?
- Are X-rays and dental exams covered?

### Coverage Limit

- What is the annual dental maximum?
- Is the limit per person or per family?
- Does the annual limit reset by calendar year or benefit year?

### Family Coverage

- Does coverage include spouse?
- Does coverage include child?
- What enrollment steps are required for dependents?

### Claim Process

- Can I pay first and claim later?
- Is direct billing supported?
- What documents are required for claim submission?
- Is a treatment plan estimate recommended before expensive dental work?

## Questions to Ask Dental Clinic

- Do they accept the Synopsys dental insurance provider?
- Do they support direct billing?
- Can they provide a pre-treatment estimate?
- What is the estimated cost of deep cleaning?
- How many appointments are required?
- Is periodontal maintenance needed afterward?

## Suggested Action Plan

1. Wait for official benefits package or HR instruction.
2. Confirm dental coverage start date.
3. Ask benefits provider whether deep cleaning / scaling and root planing is covered.
4. Ask dental clinic for treatment estimate.
5. Submit pre-determination if required.
6. Schedule treatment after coverage is active, unless medically urgent.

## Risks

- Dental coverage may not start immediately.
- Deep cleaning may have partial coverage only.
- Annual maximum may be limited.
- Pre-authorization may be required.
- Some clinics may not support direct billing.
- If treatment is done before coverage starts, claim may be rejected.
