这里选 **“request for information”**。

这是目前最合适的分类，因为你是在要求他们解释：

- 120股如何变成50股；
- 50股为何又变成15股；
- “120 rights withdrawn”到底是什么系统含义；
- 各绩效条件的最终计算结果。

暂时不要选 **claim**。`claim` 更像正式投诉或争议申诉，适合他们先给出解释后，发现计算错误或拒绝提供依据时再用。人类机构通常要先让你走一遍“请解释”，然后才允许你正式说“你们算错了”，流程设计得像迷宫一样精致。

接下来填写：

**Category:** `request for information`

**Subject：**

> Clarification of withdrawn rights under AGATE 2026 – Reference AAAG45250723

**Message：**

> Dear Sir or Madam,  
>   
> I am requesting a written explanation regarding the rights shown in my Uptevia account under the AGATE 2026 Dolphin International Plan, reference AAAG45250723.  
>   
> I was originally granted 120 performance share rights on July 25, 2023. Earlier in July 2026, my account showed 70 rights withdrawn and 50 rights in the process of being granted. The account now shows 120 rights withdrawn and only 15 rights in the process of being granted.  
>   
> Please provide:  
>   
> 1. The exact calculation resulting in 15 remaining rights.  
> 2. The reason the pending rights changed from 50 to 15.  
> 3. Whether “120 rights withdrawn” is only a technical display of the original grant or an additional withdrawal.  
> 4. The final achievement percentage app?
